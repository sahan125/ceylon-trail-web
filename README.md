# Ceylon Trail Sri Lanka — Tourist Rentals & Chauffeur Services

A high-performance Next.js 16 web application recreating the official **Ceylon Trail Sri Lanka** tourist rental and tour platform, designed specifically for international travelers arriving at Colombo Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA).

## ✨ Features

- **Top Announcement Ribbon**: 24/7 airport handover notice, AAC tourist driving permit issuing status, and direct WhatsApp hotline.
- **Header & Navigation**: Sticky navbar with custom brand crest, category links, multi-language switcher (EN, DE, FR), WhatsApp quick call, and "Get Instant Quote" trigger.
- **Hero & Fleet Search Widget**:
  - Filter by **Self-Drive Rental** or **Chauffeur Guided Tour**.
  - Dropdown for BIA Airport, Colombo Fort, Negombo, Kandy, Galle, and Ella.
  - Pickup and return date/time pickers with real-time day calculation.
  - "Cars Guaranteed or Free Upgrade" and zero card fee guarantee badges.
- **International Traveler Guarantees**:
  - No Local Driving Permit? (Automobile Association of Ceylon - AAC legal endorsement).
  - Transparent Security Deposits (Physical FX currency cash options in EUR/USD/GBP/AUD with 0% markups).
  - 24/7 Islandwide Emergency mechanical roadside recovery.
  - BIA Terminal Gate direct curbside vehicle handover.
- **Verified Tourist Japanese Fleet**:
  - Suzuki Wagon R FX (Compact Hybrid, 22 km/L) — $25/day
  - Toyota Axio Hybrid (Comfort Sedan, 19 km/L) — $38/day
  - Toyota Raize Turbo (Compact SUV, 200mm Clearance) — $45/day
  - Toyota HiAce KDH (Luxury Tour Van, Up to 10 Pax) — $65/day
  - Category filters: All Classes, Hatchbacks, Sedans, SUV, Passenger Vans.
- **Curated Scenic Tour Packages**:
  - Classic Ceylon Explorer (7 Days / 6 Nights) — $490
  - Hill Country & Tea Trails (4 Days / 3 Nights) — $280
  - Grand Island Grand Loop (10 Days / 9 Nights) — $720
- **Social Proof & Official Credentials**:
  - 4.96 / 5.0 rating across 380+ verified international tourist reviews.
  - SLTDA Tourist Transport Board Approved license (`TSL/CAR/2023/0144`).
  - Government endorsed AAC permit handling.
- **Interactive Modals**:
  - Instant Quote / Booking Calculator with live cost estimation and one-click WhatsApp pre-filled booking.
  - Tour Package itinerary and inclusions modal.
  - Driving permit document checklist modal.
  - Transparent deposit & roadside emergency information modals.
- **Floating WhatsApp Support**:
  - Fixed live chat button with active agent status.
